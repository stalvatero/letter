Privacy Policy

Letter (also shown as “Lettera”) · Effective 26 November 2026 · Developer: Salvatore Oscurato

This Privacy Policy explains how the Letter desktop email application (“Letter”, “we”, “the app”) handles information when you install and use it on your computer. Letter is an open-source personal project distributed primarily as a Flatpak and from github.com/stalvatero/letter.

1. Summary

  • Letter is a local desktop mail client. It does not run a Letter cloud mailbox service.
  • Mail, tokens, and preferences stay on your device (and, if you choose them, in your desktop account providers).
  • When you sign in with Google or Microsoft through Letter Identity, those providers authenticate you; Letter receives tokens needed to access your mail on your behalf.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not operate ads in Letter.

2. Who is responsible

Letter is developed by Salvatore Oscurato. For privacy questions, open a GitHub issue.

3. Data Letter processes on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Letter may store locally:

  • Account details you add in Letter Identity (display name, email address, provider, IMAP/SMTP settings when used).
  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) for Google or Microsoft sign-in, typically in the system secret store / keyring.
  • Mail content and metadata cached for offline use and performance (headers, message bodies, attachments you open or that are prefetched according to your preferences).
  • Preferences (theme, sync interval, folder layout, and similar settings) via desktop settings storage.
  • Optional collected addresses from mail you send, used only for compose suggestions on your device.

Local cache and accounts can be removed from Letter’s Preferences (for example by removing an Identity account and optionally deleting its local library). Uninstalling the Flatpak or clearing the app’s data directory also removes sandbox-local data.

4. Google sign-in (Letter Identity)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Letter uses Google OAuth 2.0 (PKCE / loopback) and requests scopes needed to identify you and access Gmail for IMAP/mail use in the app, including:

  • openid, email, profile
  • https://mail.google.com/ (Gmail / IMAP access required for the mail client)

Google’s own privacy policy applies to the authentication and to data Google holds in your Google Account: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Letter uses the resulting tokens only to provide mail features on your device. Letter does not receive your Google password.

5. Microsoft sign-in (Letter Identity)

If you choose Sign in with Microsoft, Letter uses Microsoft identity / Graph (or related mail APIs) according to the permissions shown on Microsoft’s consent screen. Microsoft’s privacy statement applies to that sign-in: https://privacy.microsoft.com/. Tokens are stored locally like Google tokens and used to sync and send mail from Letter on your device.

6. GNOME Online Accounts (optional)

On GNOME desktops you may enable accounts that already exist in GNOME Online Accounts / Evolution Data Server. In that mode, sign-in and some account data are managed by the desktop stack, not only by Letter. Letter reads the mail sources those services expose. You can turn this off in Letter Preferences and use Letter Identity only.

7. Network use

Letter contacts third-party servers only as needed to provide the product, for example:

  • Google or Microsoft identity and mail endpoints when you use those accounts
  • Your IMAP/SMTP host when you use a password-based IMAP account
  • Optional update checks against Letter’s GitHub Releases (version metadata), if that feature is used

Letter does not send your mailbox contents to the Letter developer for analytics or advertising.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data leaves your device toward Google, Microsoft, or your mail host only because you configured those providers and Letter must talk to them to fetch or send mail. If you open a support issue on GitHub, anything you paste there is public unless you use a private channel.

9. Retention

Letter retains local data until you delete accounts/cache, clear app data, or uninstall. Provider-side retention (Gmail, Microsoft 365, IMAP host) is controlled by those providers and your account settings with them.

10. Security

OAuth tokens are stored with the desktop secret service when available. Mail cache lives in the application’s local data directories. No method of storage is perfect; protect your user account and device encryption as you would for any mail client.

11. Children

Letter is a general-purpose email client and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use Letter Identity sign-in for such users.

12. International users

Processing happens on your computer and at the locations of your chosen mail providers. If you are in the EEA/UK, your providers’ and this policy’s descriptions are intended to help you understand the purposes of local processing (providing the mail client you requested).

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when Letter’s behaviour or legal requirements change. The “Effective” date at the top will be revised. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
